# Optional review for Delaney

Nothing on the site depends on Delaney's review: every cell is checked automatically against the
ICEB rulebook, liblouis and Unicode. The list of places where the official sources were genuinely
ambiguous is **empty**.

These are the teaching choices we made where the rules allow more than one approach. Each takes
about a minute to look at, and none is urgent.

1. **Uncontracted first.** The free course teaches uncontracted UEB (letters, capitals, numbers,
   punctuation) before contractions, and says so in the lessons. Many US programs teach contracted
   braille to children from the start. Parents learning to support a child may want to know which
   one their child's school uses. Lessons: `/learn/reading-words` and `/learn/alphabet-wordsigns`.
2. **Letters standing alone.** Following ICEB rule 5.11.1, uncontracted examples write a lone
   letter ("plan b") with no grade 1 indicator. In contracted braille the same letter would need
   one (⠰⠃), because "b" alone means "but". The lessons point this out.
3. **Capitals terminator next to punctuation.** ICEB 8.6.2 allows the terminator before or after a
   following punctuation mark. The site writes it after (as in the rulebook's examples) and never
   asks a learner to type that case or marks the other order wrong.
