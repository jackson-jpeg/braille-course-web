# Where to send the outreach note

Checked on 2026-11-17 by opening each page. **Nothing has been sent.** "Opened" means I loaded the page and read the
contact details from it. Anything I could not open is in the last section, not in the list.

## 1. American Foundation for the Blind (AFB)

- The list to be on: Resources for Teachers of Braille
  https://afb.org/blindness-and-low-vision/braille/resources-teachers-braille
  and its page Selected Resources for Teaching Braille
  https://afb.org/blindness-and-low-vision/braille/resources-teachers-braille/selected-resources-teaching-braille
- Contact page (opened): https://afb.org/about-afb/contact-afb
  It gives a phone number and postal addresses only: 212-502-7600 (general office line). No email and no form.
- An email on the Selected Resources page (opened): literacy@afb.net. It is printed there for the DOTS for Braille
  Literacy newsletter, not for suggestions, and the page looks old, so it may bounce.
- Honest view: this is the hardest of the three. The Selected Resources page lists books, slates and assessment tools
  with postal addresses, and no websites or apps. A phone call to the general line asking who looks after the braille
  pages is the realistic first step.

## 2. Paths to Literacy (run by Perkins School for the Blind)

- Submission page (opened): https://www.pathstoliteracy.org/submit-idea/
  A form (name, email, message, a file upload) and an email address: pathsto.literacy@perkins.org
- Their terms, from that page: they want a post with a title, a body and an image, sent as a Word or Google doc;
  material must be original; a submitted article becomes the property of Paths to Literacy.
- Honest view: the best fit. They publish resources and ideas from teachers. A short post by Delaney ("how I use
  practice games between braille lessons") with the links inside it suits them better than the bare note. Because
  the article becomes theirs, Delaney should decide that knowingly.
- Their FAQ also names a manager with a personal address. I did not open that page myself, so use the address above.
