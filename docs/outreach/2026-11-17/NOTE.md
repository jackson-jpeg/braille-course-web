Subject: A free braille practice site and app for your resource list

Hello,

I'm Delaney Costello, a certified Teacher of the Visually Impaired. teachbraille.org grew out of my own braille lessons, as a place for students and families to practice Unified English Braille between sessions.

Everything there is free to use, with no account: a step-by-step introduction to braille, a short course, and interactive practice games. There is also a free iPhone and iPad app, TeachBraille, with sixteen games. It works offline, has no ads or accounts, and is built for VoiceOver, large text and high contrast.

If it looks useful, I would be grateful if you would consider adding it to your braille resources. I'm happy to answer questions, and I'd love to hear what would make it more helpful for your teachers and families.

Website: https://teachbraille.org
App: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6833681749

Thank you for all you do for braille readers.

Delaney Costello
Delaney@TeachBraille.org
