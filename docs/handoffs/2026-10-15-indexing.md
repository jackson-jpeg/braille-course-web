# teachbraille.org: indexing, Search Console, funnel (2026-10-15)

## Your one action: the Search Console token

DNS for teachbraille.org is **not on Vercel** (nameservers are `ns-cloud-b1..b4.googledomains.com`), so
I can't add a TXT record. Per your instructions that means the meta-tag method, which means a **URL-prefix**
property rather than a Domain property:

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Open the property menu (top left) and choose **Add property**
3. In the right-hand box, **URL prefix**, enter `https://teachbraille.org/` and press **Continue**
4. Under **Other verification methods**, open **HTML tag**
5. Copy just the `content="..."` value from the tag and send it to me. **Leave that tab open.**

When it arrives, I add it to `app/layout.tsx`, deploy, and confirm the tag is live. You then click **Verify**
in that tab. Google's verify step has to run as your account, so that click can't be done from here.

(A Domain property would also cover www and http, but it needs a TXT record at the DNS host. The
URL-prefix property is enough, because every other URL now redirects to the apex.)
